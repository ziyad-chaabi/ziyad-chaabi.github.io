Context

Two projects from the Master’s in Metaverse Science and Technology. Funfair VR: a medieval fair with five mini-games. Hotel Murder VR: a detective escape game in a hotel, where each team member builds their own scene.

The problem

In VR there is no room for a manual. Everything has to make sense once you pick things up, with nothing to read.

What I built

  1. Funfair VR, my part

    The dunk tank, the punching bag, the VR portal menu linking the scenes, the music and the sound effects.

  2. Hotel Murder VR, the security room

    In the three-person escape game, my scene is the security room: NPCs, an investigation and a mix of puzzles.

Technical decisions

A menu you walk through
The Funfair VR menu is made of portals: you go from one attraction to the next without leaving the world.
Physics as the rules
The dunk tank and the punching bag rely on Unity physics: you aim, you throw, you hit.
Two Unity versions
Unity 2022.3 with the Universal Render Pipeline, XR Interaction Toolkit and OpenXR for Funfair VR; Unity 6 and XR Interaction Toolkit 3 for Hotel Murder VR.

  • 6scenes
